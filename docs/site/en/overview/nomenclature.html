Nomenclature & conventions reference

This page collects the vocabulary the rest of the documentation relies on: the version labels, the abbreviations, the external code and facility names, the shared physical constants of the frozen cgs + eV unit system, and a disambiguation table for symbols that carry different meanings on different pages. Kernel pages define their own symbols locally; come here when a symbol or acronym appears without a nearby definition.

Version vocabulary

This documentation describes TENRYU v1 (also written v1.0), the current production generation of the code. Lowercase “v” labels are model or feature generations, not releases of the whole code, and several kernels carry their own generation labels:

LabelMeaning
v1 / v1.0The TENRYU production generation documented by this site. Statements such as “v1 omits radiation pressure force” scope a decision to this generation.
burn v2, “Version 2”The second-generation nuclear-burn model: screening, multigroup charged-product diffusion, and Monte Carlo alpha transport.
burn v2-EThe neutron-heating extension of burn v2 (two-line first-collision model).
MPI v1The first-generation MPI port: its audited feature scope and deferral guards are listed on the MPI page.
M1 v1The first generation of the M1/TMOP rezone solver on the ALE page.
V3 (hydro)The internal feature name of the experimental, opt-in 1D solution-adaptive ALE path mentioned on the hydrodynamics page. Unrelated to the verification deck name 2d_rz_v3_smooth_temporal, which measures the midpoint_v1 convergence orders.
pc_v0, midpoint_v1, bbsw_radial_v0, …Namelist values whose suffix records the generation of that particular algorithm; older generations are retained for frozen-configuration compatibility.

Shared constants of the cgs + eV system

The unit system is frozen: lengths in cm, time in s, mass in g, energy in erg, density in g/cm³, temperatures and photon-group bounds in eV. Three conventions recur on every physics page:

  • \(k_B\) means eV_to_erg \(=1.6022\times10^{-12}\) erg/eV. Because temperatures are stored in eV, every \(k_BT\) in this documentation converts eV to erg; the Kelvin Boltzmann constant never appears.
  • \(a_{\rm eV}=1.3720\times10^{2}\) erg cm⁻³ eV⁻⁴ is the radiation constant expressed in eV units, so equilibrium radiation energy density is \(a_{\rm eV}T^4\) with \(T\) in eV.
  • \(m_p=1.6726\times10^{-24}\) g is the proton mass. Number densities use \(n_i=\rho/(A\,m_p)\) with \(A\) in amu — TENRYU deliberately treats the atomic mass unit as \(m_p\); the ≈0.8% difference from the true amu is a fixed, documented convention absorbed into the material's effective \(A\).

Symbols with more than one meaning

Kernel pages define symbols where they use them, and several letters are standard in more than one subfield. The table lists every multi-use symbol in this documentation and the page context that selects each meaning.

SymbolMeanings by context
\(\alpha\)Fleck implicitness parameter, default 1.0 (FLD); the curvilinear hoop-term geometry factor, 1 cylindrical / 2 spherical (viscosity); a Winslow metric coefficient (ALE); and in \(\alpha_{iaw}\), the CBET ion-acoustic damping rate.
\(\beta\)Radiation–matter stiffness ratio \(4a_{\rm eV}T_e^3/C_{v,e}\) (FLD); the SNB normalized electron energy \(E/(k_BT_e)\) (with \(\hat\beta\) the group-boundary values normalized by the global max \(T_e\)); a Winslow metric coefficient and the M1 barrier weight (ALE).
\(\lambda\)FLD flux-limiter function \(\lambda(R)\); SNB group stopping length \(\lambda_g\) (with abs/tr variants); the ALE line-search blend factor; and \(\lambda_0\), the laser vacuum wavelength.
\(\theta\)Conduction harmonic flux-limit factor \(1/(1+|q|/q_{max})\); the CBET donor cap theta_cap; the \(S_N\) donor-\(\theta\) export limiter; and the polar angle of polar meshes and beam geometry.
\(\kappa\)Mass opacity [cm²/g] (radiation, EOS/opacity). In the conduction context, the electron thermal conductivity (\(\kappa\propto T_e^{5/2}\)). \(\kappa_{\rm IB}\) is the inverse-bremsstrahlung absorption coefficient (laser).
\(g\)Photon- or electron-energy group index (radiation, SNB, hot electrons, burn); in CBET, the normalized detuning of the gain curve \(P(g)\) — the CBET page's tally “group” index \(g=b(2N_{bin})+\sigma N_{bin}+a\) is a third, unrelated bookkeeping index.
\(f\)The Fleck factor \(f_{c,g}\) (FLD); the conduction free-streaming fraction f_lim; the explicit-source time-step fraction \(f_E\) (explicit_source_limit); the CBET calibration factor f_cbet; the hot-electron capture fraction \(f_s\) (source_nc_fraction); the burn ion-heating fraction \(f_i\); and the CBET donor-cap factor \(f_g\).
\(R\)The FLD limiter argument \(|\nabla E_g|/(\sigma_{R,g}E_g)\); the radial coordinate in \((R,Z)\); the SNB flux-response ratio of the G2 gate; and the viscosity channel ratio \(\eta_e/\eta_i\).
\(E\)Group radiation energy density \(E_{c,g}\) (radiation pages); particle kinetic energy (hot-electron and burn group grids); conservative cell thermal energy \(E_c\) (2D conduction update); \(\mathcal E\) (script) is the electrostatic field in the SNB E-field option.
\(e\)Specific internal energy \(e_i,e_e\) [erg/g] (subscripts: ion and electron species); the elementary charge in collision-rate and critical-density formulas such as \(m_e\omega_0^2/(4\pi e^2)\); and the exponential base, as in the \(e^{-2}\) spot radius.
\(u\)Fluid velocity \(\mathbf u\); in the hot-electron spectrum equations, the normalized energy \(u=E/T_h\) (with \(u_{g\pm1/2}\) the group edges and \(u_{min},u_{max}\) the window edges).
\(k\)Boltzmann conversion in \(k_BT\) (see constants above); the perturbation wavenumber in dispersion and decay-rate formulas; \(\mathbf k\) the optical wavevector in CBET.
\(m_i\)Cell mass of cell \(i\) in the hot-electron time-step limiter; \(\bar m_i=A_{eff}m_p\) is the mean ion mass in the CBET acoustic speed.
Subscripts \(i\), \(j\)In \(T_i,e_i,P_i\), the ion species; in the 1D discretization, the cell index \(i\) (\(V_i,\Delta M_i\)) and node index \(j\) (\(r_j,u_j\)). Context — species or grid — selects the meaning.
\(M\), \(M_c\), \(\Delta M_i\)All denote Lagrangian cell mass: \(\Delta M_i\) in 1D cell-mass form, \(M_c\) per 2D cell; burn's \(M_{req}/M_{max}\) are instead subcycle counts (requested versus the subcycle_max cap).
\(\Delta l_c\)The cell size measure used by dt limits: the cell width in 1D, and \(\sqrt{A_c}\) in 2D with \(A_c\) the cell's meridian-plane area.
\(T_h\) (= \(T_{hot}\))The hot-electron exponential-spectrum temperature (T_hot_eV, converted to erg internally).
\(n_c\), \(n_{\rm crit}\)The same quantity — the laser critical density \(m_e\omega_0^2/(4\pi e^2)\); the hot-electron page writes \(n_c\), the laser page \(n_{\rm crit}\).
\(Z_{\rm eff}\), \(\bar Z_{\rm eff}\)\(Z_{\rm eff}=\bar Z r_2\) is the collisional effective charge from charge-state moments (with \(r_2=\langle Z^2\rangle/\bar Z^2\)); \(\bar Z_{\rm eff}\) (and \(A_{\rm eff}\)) are mixed-cell composition averages. The verification tag R2 is unrelated to \(r_2\).
L1 / L2Laser verification gate tiers (see the gate-tag legend); \(L_2\)/\(L_\infty\) (also \(\varepsilon_{L2}\), \(\varepsilon_{L\infty}\)) are relative error norms — root-mean-square and maximum-magnitude relative differences over a field.
\(w_m\), \(w_k\), \(w_0\)\(w_m\): \(S_N\) ordinate weights, normalized to the angular measure (the 1D Gauss–Legendre set sums to 2, \(\int_{-1}^1d\mu\), with azimuth folded into \(\psi\); hence \(\sum_{\mu_m>0}w_m\mu_m=1/2\)); \(w_k\): laser annulus ray weights (profile × exact area, normalized to 1); \(w_0\): the \(e^{-2}\) spot radius of the beam profile.
