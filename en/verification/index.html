Verification & benchmarks reference

TENRYU verification progresses from kernel gates with closed-form results, through golden regressions and reproducibility audits, to integrated 2D RZ batteries and cross-code comparisons. Higher rungs exercise more physics but do not replace the analytic gates below them. Distinct oracles and acceptance bands identify the contract that changed.

At a glance

  • Analytic gates cover Sedov and Noh hydrodynamics, the multigroup Marshak spectrum, the SNB Epperlein–Short dispersion band, and analytic laser refraction and absorption laws.
  • The GXII 1D FLD regression compares implosion history metrics with a golden. Goldens are re-baselined only for justified physics or numerical changes, with quantitative review.
  • Deterministic 1D paths require run-to-run bitwise identity. Selected 2D RZ paths treat atomicAdd ordering at the least-significant-bit level through explicit noise-band contracts.
  • The I-series raises grey radiative shocks, multigroup FLD collapse, and SN radiative-shock ladders into coupled 2D RZ acceptance batteries.

Verification ladder

The ladder accumulates evidence in order: one operator, a frozen configuration, execution reproducibility, multi-operator coupling, and comparison with another implementation. Small gates localize sign, unit, coefficient, and boundary-condition errors. Integrated batteries ask whether those parts remain consistent in the production time loop, mesh, and energy ledgers. Cross-code results support validation; they do not replace internal verification.

Analytic and unit-gate catalog

An analytic gate owns an oracle that a wrong implementation would not reproduce. Where practical, its reference avoids production helpers and uses an independent integral, discrete eigenvalue, or published solution.

AreaReference and principal observablesRepresentative gate names
HydrodynamicsSedov–Taylor compares shock radius, density profile, and energy with the similarity solution. Noh checks shock position, compression plateau, upstream flow, and conservation in each geometry.sedov, noh, noh_planar, noh_cylindrical
Multigroup FLDAn eight-group Marshak plateau is compared with a spectrum from independent Planck integration. Frequency-dependent relaxation checks group-opacity wiring through a closed-form backward-Euler response.fld_1d_mg_planar_marshak_spectrum, fld_1d_mg_planar_freqdep_relaxation
SNB conductionA tight comparison with the discrete dispersion relation is separated from a physical band based on Epperlein–Short / Vlasov–Fokker–Planck (VFP) kinetic data [4,5]. Grid and group ladders, monotonicity, the local limit, and conservation complete the suite.snb_local_limit_1d, snb_dispersion_1d, snb_conservation_1d
Laser / opacityBeer–Lambert attenuation in uniform matter, the turning point in an analytic refractive-index profile, 3D-ray absorption, and the deposition ledger expose errors such as missing or double application of rho*kappa.beer_lambert, laser_3d_beer_lambert, laser_3d_refraction
Radiation frontThe Hammer–Rosen supersonic Marshak wave [3] compares front isotherms and the incoming/escaping energy ledger with the perturbative analytic solution.hammer_rosen_supersonic

Tolerances are not uniform. A deterministic Beer–Lambert trace has no sampling noise and uses a tight relative error. The SNB band from published data remains separate from the operator gate; its high-wavenumber model limit is not certified by widening the band.

The GXII benchmark

Throughout this documentation, GXII is GEKKO XII — the 12-beam Nd:glass laser facility at the Institute of Laser Engineering, The University of Osaka. TENRYU's standing application benchmark is a GXII-equivalent direct-drive implosion: twelve beams arranged on the faces of a regular dodecahedron (implemented as the twelve icosahedron-vertex directions) irradiate a 500 µm capsule. The golden-regression deck examples/verification/gxii_1d_fld_regression.py freezes this configuration:

ItemValue in the regression deck
TargetCH shell (\(\rho=1.05\) g/cm³, 20 µm thick, outer radius 250 µm; \(A=6.5\), \(Z=3.5\)) around DT gas (0.010 g/cm³; \(A=2.5\), \(Z=1\)); VOID exterior.
Seed coronaExponential blowoff \(\rho=0.05\,e^{-(r-250\,\mu m)/2\,\mu m}\) g/cm³ over 10 µm with a \(3\times10^{-4}\) g/cm³ tail guard, placing the 351 nm CH critical density (0.028 g/cm³) inside a resolved ramp.
Drive10 kJ in a 1 ns square pulse (10 TW total, 10 ps ramps) at \(\lambda=351\) nm (3ω); 12 beams at f/3 with super-Gaussian spots (\(w_0=200\) µm, \(m=4\)), 1000 rays per beam, chord raytrace.
PhysicsTwo-temperature Lagrangian hydrodynamics, Spitzer–Härm conduction, 20-group FLD (log-spaced bounds 0.01–100 eV, Levermore–Pomraning limiter); ideal-gas EOS (\(\gamma=5/3\)), constant opacity \(\kappa=100\) cm²/g, fixed full-ionization \(\bar Z\). CBET and hot electrons off.

Kernel pages that quote “GXII” conditions — golden absorption, corona substep counts, S8/S16 comparisons, “GXII-class” Studio presets — refer to this deck or a stated variant of it. Its target and drive parameters double as the physical definition wherever “GXII-class” appears.

Golden regression and re-baselining

verify_gxii_1d_fld_regression covers a 1D spherical implosion with laser deposition, hydrodynamics, conduction, two-temperature matter, and FLD. Its history metrics are absorbed laser energy, bang time, rho_peak_max, areal density rhoR_max, and central temperature Tc_max. The contract requires rho_peak_max within 10% of its golden, bang time within 5%, and other gated metrics within their stated tolerances. shell_r_min is informational. A mismatched run_profile (the record of run conditions such as the grid and run length) fails the comparison itself, so a shortened run or a different grid cannot be passed off as meeting the same goldens.

A golden detects changes on the same grid and run profile; it does not prove exactness or grid convergence. Re-baselining requires a known bug fix, a physics-algorithm or baseline-parameter change, or code-owner approval. The update must state the reason, quantify old-to-new differences, provide convergence data across resolutions, and receive review. A failing run alone is not a rationale.

Reproducibility gates and the 2D exception

Reproducibility contracts are named by tier. These tags appear across the kernel pages:

TierContract
T-bitBitwise identity between replicas (and across rank counts where declared).
T-sumOnly MPI reduction order may differ; normalized differences are gated, normally at \(10^{-8}\).
T-noiseThe documented 2D RZ atomicAdd least-significant-bit mechanism; acceptance is a deck- and metric-specific band self-calibrated from replicas or frozen from an ensemble.
OFF-bitWith a feature disabled or never triggered, the run must be bitwise identical to the path without the feature.
EnvelopeA wider per-deck worst-case bound calibrated from reference-run ensembles, used for metrics with documented nondeterminism.

Deterministic 1D runs with the same binary, input, and GPU configuration must produce bitwise-identical fields and diagnostics. GXII 1D uses per-ray private laser deposition followed by fixed-order reduction; the complete trajectory must reproduce bitwise. Physical tolerances retained for toolchain robustness do not permit same-configuration replica drift.

Some 2D RZ corner-force and tally paths use atomicAdd from many threads. Their addition order may vary and inject a difference in the least significant bits. For this documented exception, bitwise equality would be the wrong oracle: acceptance uses a self-calibrated band from same-configuration replicas or a frozen ensemble band. Long-run radial-invariance checks separate a clean first-step audit from the terminal difference, which must lie inside a growth band appropriate to the run length. This is a metric- and deck-specific T-noise contract, not a general tolerance. Deterministic intermediates and 1D legs remain bitwise requirements.

I-series integrated 2D RZ batteries

The I-series promotes reference problems into the coupled production path. Each harness executes angular, opacity, grid, or solver rungs and passes only when every required item in its summary JSON closes.

The coupled I1 row and the full I2 and I3 acceptance tests include strict in their names: they evaluate the complete mandatory harness rather than a partial smoke. Their Catch2 [.expensive] tag is exported as the CTest expensive label, keeping long GPU batteries separate from the short development loop. The label classifies runtime and campaign scale; it does not weaken acceptance.

Gate-tag legend

Kernel pages abbreviate their gate families with short tags. The tags are names, not a strength ranking. “Rungs” are the levels of one battery's internal ladder (for example an angular-resolution ladder), and “tier-1” marks a deliberately reduced first-generation model contract.

Tag familyMeaning
L1 / L2 (laser)Laser gate tiers: L1 = single-physics laser closure contracts (refraction, Beer–Lambert, energy ledger, ray-count convergence); L2 = laser+hydro coupled contracts. Unrelated to the \(L_2\)/\(L_\infty\) error norms.
G1–G4 (conduction)The SNB nonlocal-conduction gate series: G1 OFF-bit against the local model, G2 local limit and quadratic asymptote, G3 Epperlein–Short dispersion, G4 conservation.
G-R / G-K / G-P / G-L (burn)Burn gate series by area: R = reactivities and depletion, K = Fraley range/kernel, P = electron–ion partition, L = energy ledgers.
B1 / B2 / N1 / W1–W8 (viscosity)Braginskii-viscosity batteries: B = coefficient, strain, and decay gates; N1 = the curvature-geometry mode gate; W = the electron-channel and 2D parity port series.
H1 / H2 (2D hydro)2D RZ hydro reference decks: H1 planar Sod, H2 cylindrical Noh.

HELIOS 1D cross-code benchmark

For LTE (local-thermodynamic-equilibrium) comparison with HELIOS — a commercial 1D Lagrangian radiation-hydrodynamics code from Prism Computational Sciences — matched-irradiation cases, rather than merely equal nominal laser power, were established as the meaningful basis: a ≈113 µm Gaussian spot delivered by a nearly collimated f/191 beam, with LTE table opacities on both sides. Across those matched cases, measured absorbed fractions were about 45% in TENRYU and about 55–73% in HELIOS, with attribution work recorded for the difference. These numbers are deliberately not comparable with the golden deck's 83–84% absorption: the golden's twelve f/3 beams with 200 µm super-Gaussian spots overfill the 250 µm-radius target, a much broader drive geometry (the Examples page collects the three absorption figures and their conditions). This comparison alone does not establish that either implementation is correct or validated against experiment.

How to run

CTest selects registered gates from the build tree by pattern; tenryu verify invokes one verification driver directly.

ctest --test-dir build -R <pattern>
./build/tenryu verify <name>

# Examples
ctest --test-dir build -R "sedov|noh|snb|gxii_1d_fld"
./build/tenryu verify snb_dispersion_1d

Use ctest --test-dir build -N to list available names. Select long batteries explicitly with -L expensive, or omit them from a routine pass with -LE expensive. Gates depend on build options and, in some cases, HDF5, MPI, and a usable GPU. Do not interpret a skip as a pass; retain the CTest labels and execution log with the result.
