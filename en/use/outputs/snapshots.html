Snapshots reference

A snapshot <name>_NNNN.h5 is an HDF5 file that holds the spatial fields at one time. This page lists the root attributes and the main datasets of each group (metadata/, mesh/, hydro/, diagnostics/, radiation/, and laser/). File naming and cadence are on the Outputs & analysis page; the scalar diagnostics in time are on the History page. The canonical source including dtypes and array shapes is docs/SPECIFICATION.md §7 in the repository (summarized in docs/OUTPUT_SCHEMA.md).

Snapshot datasets

Root attributes: t (time [s]), dt (Δt [s]), cycle and step (both are the step number), geometry ("1D_SPH"|"2D_RZ"), nr and nz (Mesh.nr and, in 2D, Mesh.nz; nz is 1 in 1D), n_cells, n_nodes, n_groups, n_materials, two_temperature (always 1), schema_version. When a restart is not an exact continuation of the original run (for example, when the checkpoint has no state file for Numerics.ale.band_ale, the file named like the checkpoint with .band-state appended), the run writes a mark to the string root attribute restart_provenance (for example, RESTART_WITHOUT_BAND_STATE). Because geometry reads "1D_SPH" for every 1D geometry, 1D files also carry geometry_1d ("spherical"|"cylindrical"|"planar"). In 1D, arrays are flat and mesh/x_z, v_z are omitted. Group by group ("optional" = written conditionally):

metadata/ — provenance

DatasetContent
namelist_sourceThe executed deck (full Python source).
frozen_configFrozen-configuration JSON (for the difference from config/<name>_frozen.json, see the provenance section); attributes build_type (Release or Debug), gpu_name (the name of GPU 0), and rng_seed (the value of Main.seed). git_hash and cuda_arch are always "unknown" and n_ranks is always 1, so none of them identifies a run.
group_bounds_eVRadiation energy group edges [eV] (length G+1).

mesh/ — nodes and connectivity

DatasetContent
x_r, x_zNode coordinates [cm]; x_z 2D only.
v_r, v_zNode velocities [cm/s]; v_z 2D only.
cell_material_idRepresentative material index per cell (largest volume fraction; ties go to the smaller index).
topology/v2/, v3/, v4/optional. Multiblock mesh connectivity (mesh topology; v2: 3-block; v3: half-butterfly 5-block — block/seam tables plus CSR (compressed sparse row) connectivity; v4: pentagon-belt cell_nverts with a corner_stride attribute). Omitted for single-block meshes. See 2D meshes for the configuration details.

hydro/ — cell-centered state

DatasetContent
rho, Te, TiDensity [g/cm³], electron/ion temperatures [eV].
ee, eiElectron/ion specific internal energies [erg/g].
Pe, Pi, QviscElectron/ion pressures and artificial-viscosity pressure [dyne/cm²].
mass, volCell mass [g] and volume [cm³].
zbarMean ionization.
volFracMaterial volume fractions [N_cell, N_mat] (multi-material).
burn_rate, burn_Q_e, burn_Q_i, burn_eps_cum, burn_n_*optional (nuclear burn on, Burn.enabled=True). Reaction-rate density [1/cm³/s], electron and ion heating rates by the burn products [erg/cm³/s], cumulative specific deposited energy [erg/g], and the D, T, He3, He4, and p number densities [1/cm³]. 1D files also carry burn_neutron_cum, the cumulative number of neutrons born in each cell.
shock_timeoptional. Time of last detected shock [s] (post-shock features active).
adaptive_av_gate, adaptive_av_mode_celloptional. The per-cell weight g_i of the adaptive artificial viscosity (AV) (the dataset name says gate; dimensionless), and a visualization mask that is 1 in the cells with g_i > 10-6 (with Numerics.hydro.adaptive_av.enabled=True; 1D, av_type="vnr" only).
corner_massoptional. 2D subzonal corner masses (definition) [g] (Lagrangian invariant).
eta_compatibleoptional. Legacy volume-form compatibility mismatch diagnostic [cm³].
per_material/v1/optional. Per-material mass mass [g] and electron and ion internal energies Ee, Ei [erg], one value per cell and material (Numerics.materials.per_material_conservation_enabled=True).
gas_tracer_Yoptional. The hotspot-gas tracer Y_g (term; dimensionless, per cell), written by a run that initialized the tracer with Numerics.diagnostics.hotspot_gas.enabled=True. A run restarted from a checkpoint reads it back and continues.

diagnostics/ — snapshot-side physics diagnostics (optional)

GroupTrigger and content
areal_density/v1/Diagnostics.areal_density.enabled=True. Angle list angles_deg and areal densities rhoR [g/cm²]; optional rhoR_hotspot_tracer (the integral ∫ρY_g ds of the density times the hotspot-gas tracer Y_g; term).
hotspot_gas/v1/Numerics.diagnostics.hotspot_gas.enabled=True. Te/Ti statistics of the hotspot gas (term; mass-weighted mean/p10/p50/p90; Ti only in two-temperature runs), energies (internal/kinetic/total plus initial values), work proxies, and the definition string hotspot_work_definition.

radiation/ — radiation field and diagnostics

DatasetContent
energy_densityGroup radiation energy density E_g [erg/cm³] ([N_cell, G]).
rad_dep, rad_emitPer-step accumulated absorption and emission [erg] (per group).
deposited_powerAbsorbed power density [erg/cm³/s] (= rad_dep/(VΔt); for visualization).
fleck_factor2D flux-limited diffusion (FLD) only: per-cell Fleck factor. The diagnostic is meaningful only for gray G=1.
sn_tau_R, sn_reduced_flux, sn_ap_alpha2D SN (discrete ordinates) only: diagnostics of the asymptotic-preserving (AP) blend of SN and diffusion face fluxes (optical-depth, reduced-flux, and blend-weight proxies).
diag_* (12 kinds)1D SN output-only diagnostics: diag_rad_ followed by E_pre/E_post/emission_at_Tn/emission_at_Tnp1/absorption, and diag_ followed by clip_energy/clip_full_deficit/chi_opacity/F_first_moment/E_star_flux/stream_theta/ap_alpha_face. Never used to restore the state at a restart.

laser/ — laser (only with Laser.enabled=True)

DatasetContent
deposited_powerLaser deposition power density [erg/cm³/s].
absorption_fractionOverall absorption fraction: the cumulative absorbed energy divided by the sum of the cumulative absorbed energy, unabsorbed energy, and E_cbet_iaw; it equals absorbed/incident when no laser energy was dropped for lack of a receiver.
E_ra, E_cbet_iaw, E_cbet_iaw_stepScalars [erg]: the cumulative laser energy absorbed by resonance absorption, and the signed cumulative energy that CBET (cross-beam energy transfer) gave to the ion-acoustic wave (IAW) with its value for this step. The same quantities as the history channels energy/laser_ra_deposited, energy/E_cbet_iaw, and energy/E_cbet_iaw_step.
ray_densityInitial ray placement density [1/cm³].
A_Q_r_shells, A_Q_z_shells2D, single-block meshes only (not written for multiblock meshes): deposition asymmetry \((q_{max}-q_{min})/(q_{max}+q_{min})\) per cell line (A_Q_r_shells: a z-direction column of cells at fixed r index; A_Q_z_shells: an r-direction row of cells at fixed z index; "shells" in the dataset names means these lines, not the shell of the capsule). q is the laser energy [erg] deposited in each cell during that step.
mesh/Only on non-skip steps (= steps that executed the ray tracing; not written on the thinned reuse steps of Laser.raytrace_skip) with ray_output_trajectory=True: node coordinates of the LaserMesh (the laser-dedicated independent grid — a 2D RZ structured grid separate from the hydro mesh; see Laser), \(\hat n_e\) (electron density over the critical density), T_e, Z̄, density gradients, n_crit.
rays/Only on non-skip steps with ray_output_count>0: initial ray positions/directions/powers (1D: R0/Z0/vR0/vZ0; 2D: x0/y0/z0/vx0/vy0/vz0) and beam IDs.
rays/trajectory/With ray_output_trajectory=True: CSR-format trajectories (offsets, step counts, position columns, residual power) — the source data for ray overlays.

Under mode="radial_absorption_1d" there are no physical ray trajectories: deposited_power and absorption_fraction are written as usual but the rays/ family is empty or absent. The outputs of the retired Monte Carlo radiation (radiation/ddmc_flag, radiation/delta_E_rad_prev, holo/, difference/) are no longer written from schema 2 (2026-09-29); files written before may still hold them.
