Physics kernels

Each kernel page documents the physical model, the discretization actually implemented, the namelist controls, and the verification that gates it. “Kernel” here means a documented operator page: nine physics operators plus the mesh-generation and MPI-decomposition infrastructure they run on. The catalog below follows the operator ordering of a time step.

Lagrangian hydrodynamics

Compatible staggered hydrodynamics, time integration, and shock capturing.

Meshes & geometry

1D zoning and regions, 2D RZ mesh families, and ALE rezoning and remap.

Radiation transport

Multigroup FLD with Fleck-linearized implicit matter coupling, and discrete-ordinates SN with iterative Picard matter closure.

Electron & ion conduction

Local Spitzer-Härm and nonlocal SNB thermal transport.

Laser drive & absorption

Refracted ray tracing and inverse-bremsstrahlung deposition.

Cross-beam energy transfer (CBET)

Pairwise beam coupling with detuning and wave-action accounting.

Hot electrons

Laser-generated source models, angular transport, and deposition.

EOS, opacity & ionization

Analytic or tabulated material closures and ionization models.

Nuclear burn

Fusion reactions, charged-particle partition, and neutron heating.

Plasma viscosity

Braginskii momentum diffusion with stability controls.

MPI decomposition

Owned windows, ghost exchange, and deterministic reductions.
