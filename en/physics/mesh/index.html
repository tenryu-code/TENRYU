Meshes & geometry

TENRYU constructs its domain as a 1D radial grid or a 2D axisymmetric RZ mesh. The 1D path provides uniform and graded zoning with spherical, cylindrical, or planar measures; deck-side region painting assigns materials, a pre-plasma corona, and exterior VOID. The 2D path maps structured rectangular, spherical-polar, polar-in-box, and CSR-connected (compressed-sparse-row) multiblock families onto one RZ geometry contract.

At a glance

  • 1D nodes are cell boundaries, cells hold thermodynamic state, and the coordinate measure is spherical, cylindrical, or planar.
  • uniform gives equal spacing; graded uses per-segment cell weights and boundary matching.
  • Studio can generate concentric volume-fraction regions, VOID excluded from conduction and radiation, and a monotone laser-target corona ramp.
  • The 2D default is a structured quadrilateral mesh. Polar, box embedding, shell/collar, and pentagon-belt forms are opt-in logical or multiblock families.

Mesh-quality guards

Builders fail loudly on incomplete segment coverage, non-monotone nodes, failed exact polar-axis snaps, wrong cell orientation, or nonpositive RZ volume. During evolution, minimum altitude and corner Jacobian \(J\) monitor cell collapse and corner inversion as distinct failure modes. A predicted approach to either floor can limit the timestep, and a candidate state that crosses a gate is rejected. These are admissibility contracts, not physics models: they prevent volume, force, and remap operators from consuming invalid geometry.
